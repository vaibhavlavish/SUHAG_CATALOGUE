Privacy Policy

This website is a photo catalogue of our in-store collection. It does not have a login or a general contact form, and browsing the collection itself does not collect any personal information about you.

Information You Provide

If you place an order, a "Delivery Details" form asks for your name, phone number, delivery address, and optionally your email. This information is used only to prepare a WhatsApp message with your order and details already filled in — it is not saved, stored, or transmitted to any server by this website. It only reaches us once you send that WhatsApp message yourself.

Information Kept On Your Own Device

This site uses your browser's local storage (not cookies) for two things only: remembering items you've added to your cart between visits, and remembering which items you've already opened, so our popularity count doesn't count the same visitor twice. Both stay on your own device — we cannot see or access either of these remotely.

Anonymous View Counts

We keep an anonymous count of how many times each item's photos are opened, so we can see which pieces get the most interest. This only records a number per item — it does not identify who viewed it, and is never linked to your name, phone number, or any other personal detail.

Third-Party Links

When you tap a WhatsApp or phone link on this site, you leave this website and go directly into WhatsApp or your phone's dialer — any conversation, photo, or number you share from that point on is handled entirely within WhatsApp, under WhatsApp's own privacy terms, not ours. If you choose to leave a review through the "Write a Review" link, that also happens entirely on Google's own review system — we don't receive or store that information on this website either.

Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children, and since it collects no information at all unless you actively choose to submit an order, there is no personal data from children for us to hold in the first place.

Security & Your Rights

Because we don't store any personal information on a server, there's nothing held by us that you'd need to request access to, correct, or delete — an order's details exist only in the WhatsApp message you send us, which is entirely within your own control from that point on. If you ever have a question or concern about how this site handles information, contact us using the details below.

Refund & Exchange Policy

Reporting an Issue

If an item you've purchased arrives damaged or defective, or isn't what you expected based on what was shown to you, please let us know within 5 days of receiving it — either over WhatsApp or in-store. We'll look into it and let you know how we can help.

Exchange Window

We offer exchange only, within 5 days from the date of your purchase. After 5 days, we're unable to offer an exchange of any kind.

Exchange Eligibility

  • The item must be unused and in the same condition you received it.
  • The item must be returned with its original box/packaging.

Once an item is brought in for exchange, we'll inspect it before confirming — the exchange is only finalised once we've verified the item meets these conditions.

No Refunds

We do not offer refunds under any circumstances. Eligible returns are exchanged only, for another item or store credit as agreed with us at the time.
