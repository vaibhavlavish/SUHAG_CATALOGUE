Privacy Policy

This website is a photo catalogue of our in-store collection. It does not have any login, checkout, or contact form, and does not collect, store, or share any personal information about visitors.

When you tap a WhatsApp or phone link on this site, you leave this website and go directly into WhatsApp or your phone's dialer — any conversation, photo, or number you share from that point on is handled entirely within WhatsApp, under WhatsApp's own privacy terms, not ours.

If you choose to leave a review through the "Write a Review" link, that also happens entirely on Google's own review system — we don't receive or store that information on this website either.

Terms of Service

This website exists to help you browse our current in-stock collection and its prices. It is not a store: nothing can be purchased, paid for, or checked out on this website itself.

  • Prices shown reflect current rates and are indicative — final pricing is confirmed at the time of sale.
  • An item shown here is not reserved or held for you until confirmed directly with us over WhatsApp or in-store.
  • All sales are completed either in person at our store or over WhatsApp, and are subject to the terms agreed at that time.

Shipping Policy

We do not offer website checkout or automated shipping. If a purchase made over WhatsApp or in-store needs to be shipped, delivery timelines, courier, and charges (if any) are discussed and agreed directly with you at the time of that sale.

Refund & Exchange Policy

Since all sales happen in-store or over WhatsApp rather than through this website, our standard in-store return, exchange, and refund terms apply — these are explained to you at the time of purchase and printed on your invoice.

If anything is unclear about a purchase you've made, please reach out to us directly and we'll be happy to help.
